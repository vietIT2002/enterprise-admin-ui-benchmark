# New employee onboarding form (HRM)

## Prompt (EN)
Build the new-employee onboarding form for our HR system: personal details, job and contract, bank and tax information, emergency contact, and documents. Make it professional.

## Prompt (VI)
Làm form tiếp nhận nhân viên mới cho hệ thống nhân sự: thông tin cá nhân, công việc và hợp đồng, ngân hàng và thuế, người liên hệ khẩn cấp, và giấy tờ. Giao diện chuyên nghiệp.

## Acceptance checklist
- [ ] The form is split into titled sections (personal, job and contract, bank and tax, emergency contact, documents) in a single column, with a visible label above every field and no placeholder-only labels
- [ ] Required fields are marked consistently, and optional fields are labelled as optional or left unmarked by a stated convention
- [ ] Field widths match their content: dates, postal codes and tax codes are narrow; inputs use the right types (email, tel, date)
- [ ] An option to save the form as a draft is offered, and saving a draft does not demand every required field
- [ ] Save and Cancel stay reachable while scrolling (a sticky bar or equivalent), Save is a verb label and is not disabled before submitting
- [ ] Fields validate on blur and on submit; with several errors a summary at the top links to each field and focus moves to the first invalid field
- [ ] Leaving the page with unsaved input triggers a warning
- [ ] The document upload states accepted file types and the size limit, lists chosen files with a remove button, and is keyboard operable
- [ ] Success is confirmed with a message that names the employee and is announced to assistive technology
