# Team leave calendar (HRM)

## Prompt (EN)
Build the team leave calendar for our HR system: who is off and when, by month and by week, including requests still waiting for approval. Keep it professional.

## Prompt (VI)
Làm lịch nghỉ phép của nhóm cho hệ thống nhân sự: ai nghỉ, nghỉ ngày nào, xem theo tháng và theo tuần, gồm cả các đơn đang chờ duyệt. Giao diện chuyên nghiệp.

## Acceptance checklist
- [ ] Month and week views, a Today button and previous/next navigation, with the visible range written as text (such as "November 2026")
- [ ] Today is marked, and weekends and public holidays are distinguishable by text or a legend, not by colour alone
- [ ] Each leave shows the person and leave type in text, and pending requests differ from approved ones by text or a pattern, not by colour alone
- [ ] A leave of several days spans its days continuously, and a crowded day shows "+N more" that opens the full list instead of hiding entries
- [ ] Days where many people are off are flagged with a count (such as "3 of 8 off")
- [ ] Opening a leave shows its absolute dates, its length in working days and its status, with approve and reject actions for pending requests
- [ ] The calendar is keyboard operable (arrow keys in the grid, or every entry reachable in tab order), and entries have accessible names that include the date
- [ ] Filters by team, person or leave type, and a list view as an alternative to the grid
- [ ] Weeks start on the locale's first day, dates follow the locale, and empty, loading and error states are present
