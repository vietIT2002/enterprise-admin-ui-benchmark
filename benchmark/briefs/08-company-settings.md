# Company settings (B2B SaaS admin)

## Prompt (EN)
Create the company settings page for a B2B SaaS admin: general information, localization (currency, timezone, date format), notifications, and a danger zone.

## Prompt (VI)
Tạo trang cài đặt công ty cho trang quản trị B2B SaaS: thông tin chung, bản địa hóa (tiền tệ, múi giờ, định dạng ngày), thông báo và vùng nguy hiểm.

## Acceptance checklist
- [ ] A left navigation groups the settings sections
- [ ] Each section uses one save model consistently (per-section save); autosave is used only for toggles and says so
- [ ] Localization settings show a formatted preview of a date and an amount
- [ ] Every toggle has a visible label and states what it controls
- [ ] The danger zone is separated at the end and its destructive action requires typing the company name or an equally strong confirmation
- [ ] Success is confirmed briefly; errors appear inline next to the field and persist
